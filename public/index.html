Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Which businesses can work with VAMSEE?

VAMSEE serves two connected needs: logistics operations solutions for logistics and fulfilment workflows, and customer support/business process services for businesses across sectors. We scope each engagement around the client’s process and requirements.

Can VAMSEE manage our complete customer support process?

Managed support can be considered after we understand your channels, volumes, operating hours, languages, systems and service expectations. Scope and coverage are agreed before any engagement.

Does VAMSEE use AI to respond to customers automatically?

AI can assist with classification and draft responses. Customer-facing replies should be reviewed and approved by an authorised person unless a client separately approves a carefully defined automation.

How do we get started?

Contact us with a short description of your process and the support challenges you want to solve. We’ll arrange a discussion and define whether a pilot is suitable.
