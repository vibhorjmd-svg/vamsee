Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Who is VAMSEE designed for?

VAMSEE is being launched for logistics businesses such as courier companies and 3PLs, and e-commerce or D2C sellers managing post-purchase shipment enquiries.

Does VAMSEE connect to every courier automatically?

Carrier integrations depend on each provider’s API access and credentials. The current workspace supports shipment records and operational event updates; provider integrations are planned as they are enabled.

Does AI send replies directly to customers?

No. AI suggestions help classify enquiries and draft replies. A human should review and approve every customer response.
